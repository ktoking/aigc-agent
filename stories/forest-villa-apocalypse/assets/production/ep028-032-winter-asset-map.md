## Generation Guardrails

- Do not use more than three shared scene/prop references in a 15-second request; add a first or last frame only when continuity needs it.
- Do not use a scene image to lock a character face. Digital-human assets lock faces; each request still states the outfit.
- For EP030-033, use `FVA_WINTER_OUTER_HEAT_DRAIN_ICE_WALL_001`, not `FVA_WINTER_EAST_ICE_BARRICADE_001`: the ice wall remains entirely outside the perimeter and blocks only the wall-root heat outlet and coolant access. It never enters the courtyard or touches the main villa.
- The turret is singular in every shot. Do not duplicate it in wide angles, reflections, or monitor feeds.
- For EP034, all ice-flow, icefall, and ice-spike effects remain outside the perimeter; they never enter the courtyard or touch the villa. Use the three-view asset as the architecture source of truth, not a text-only villa description.
- Ice woman uses digital-human asset `asset-20260720212023-wwndz` with `FVA_YE_NINGSHUANG_OUTFIT_001`: silver-white long hair, white fitted long coat, black fitted inner layer, black tights, and white anti-slip short boots. Effects references never lock her face.
