## 参考输入

- 主参考图: `01-locked-reference/main-reference.png`

## Prompt

Use the provided battle image as the locked scene reference. Preserve its ruined wet neon delivery-city atmosphere; do not invent a new location style.

Create a production environment reference sheet for `SCENE_RUINED_DELIVERY_CITY`, the same city seen in the reference image, with 4 consistent scene panels and one palette strip.

Scene lock: post-apocalyptic futuristic delivery city at night, wet reflective asphalt, cold blue-gray rain haze, collapsed overpass overhead, broken rebar silhouettes, ruined narrow street canyon, abandoned convenience-store frontage, damaged delivery lockers, distant apartment tower, patrol drones with red scanner eyes and white spotlights, occasional orange sparks and fire in the deep background. Keep the city recognizable across all panels as the same location family.

Required panels:

1. Main chase street with collapsed overpass and wet road reflections.
2. Tighter alley combat zone with broken storefronts and delivery lockers.
3. Drone searchlight view from above with red scanner lights and blue haze.
4. Quiet destination doorway or apartment entrance with faint warm meal-box glow reflected on wet floor.

Style: premium 3D anime cinematic sci-fi wasteland, hard-surface ruins, high-detail lighting, readable composition, atmospheric but not overly cluttered.

Lighting and color lock: cold blue, gray-black, alarm red; warm orange only from fire, sparks, or meal-box glow; memory blue-white as small accents. Wet ground reflections are important.

Layout: four rectangular panels in a 2 by 2 grid, no large text, clean reference sheet feel, include a small bottom palette strip with color swatches only.

Consistency constraints: all panels must feel like the same ruined delivery city from the reference. Do not switch to desert, forest, bright daytime, generic fantasy ruins, clean futuristic lab, or ordinary modern street. No new protagonist redesigns; the robot may appear only as a tiny scale silhouette if needed, not as the focus.

Negative prompts: bright daytime, clean city, fantasy magic, dense unreadable signage, overdecorated cyberpunk ads, random logos, giant monsters, gore, inconsistent environment, different cities per panel, overly dark black image, blurry background.
