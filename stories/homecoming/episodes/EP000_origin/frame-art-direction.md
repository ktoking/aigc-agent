# EP000 Frame Art Direction

## Locked visual grade

- Bright, clean overcast Japanese morning; pale grey-blue exterior and warm beige interiors.
- Rain leaves a thin reflective film on pavement, never grime, sludge, trash, soot, or a rubble field.
- In Segment 1, preserve the central gag in every applicable frame: the apartment is maintained and warmly lit while, through the window, car collisions, a crashed bus, damaged storefronts, firelight, and grey smoke make the changing city unmistakable.
- Later segments show localized city ruins: collapsed facades, damaged vehicles, inactive tanks, smoke, and small fires. Keep the foreground pavement readable and rain-washed so the world does not become a single dark, dirty rubble texture.
- The protagonist is ordinary and clean: white home T-shirt indoors, then a loose khaki overshirt over the white T-shirt, charcoal trousers, white sneakers, and white wired earbuds outdoors. He carries no grocery tote in EP000.
- Unease comes from the distant giant silhouette, unusual light, the escalating urban damage, and the contrast with the protagonist's ordinary routine. Do not make the episode uniformly dark.

## Local delivery

Each segment's `frames/` directory contains ten 16:9 PNG keyframes with scene, action, and transition coverage. They are the only image paths needed by a downstream video submission for this episode.
